The beginnings

A craft passed down three generations.

Cirilo Salilican grew up on the water, the son of a fishing family fascinated by the vessels that carried them out to sea. That fascination became a discipline. In 1957 he founded CES Craft Philippines, building boats so precisely engineered they began winning international powerboat races, drawing clients from the UAE, Japan, Australia, Singapore, and the United States.

“CES Craft. Boat Shop. Salilican. They are all one and the same.”

In 1975, Cirilo incorporated the company with his wife and children as partners. Today, as The Boat Shop Corporation, it remains family-run: a boatyard in Rosario, Cavite where clients work directly with the builders, from the drawing board to the construction yard.
